About Samboy Janta Party

Built around the people.

Samboy Janta Party was founded to listen to the wishes and demands of the public and work toward a fair, inclusive and unjudging society.

Founded 14 August 2026
Our Story

A community where people are heard.

Samboy Janta Party (SJP) was founded on 14 August 2026 under the leadership of its founding president, Samboy.

The party was created with a simple purpose: to fulfill the wishes and demands of the public while providing an environment where people can express themselves without unfair judgment.

SJP believes a community becomes stronger when its people have a voice. The organization aims to listen to its members, understand their concerns and encourage everyone to participate in shaping the community.

Our Goals & Core Values

What SJP stands for.

🗣️ Public Voice

Listen to and represent the opinions, wishes and demands of the community.

🤝 Inclusion

Create a welcoming community where everyone can participate.

⚖️ Equality

Treat members fairly and encourage equal opportunity to be heard.

The Future of SJP
